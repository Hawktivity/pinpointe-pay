Become an ambassador

Ambassador terms
  1. You must be 18 or over, and invited by GroupDarts.
  2. Once a day (UK time), your own share of a new game is covered, up to £5, when at least one other person in your group pays the rest by card. It doesn’t cover extra time, joining a game that’s already running, the free 15 or vouchers.
  3. The covered share is just for you. Use your own phone and your own Face ID or fingerprint, and don’t let anyone else use them for it.
  4. When you post about GroupDarts, say you’re an ambassador (for example with #ad). Don’t promote it to under-18s, and don’t tie it to drinking offers.
  5. We can change or end the programme, or your place on it, at any time, and we will if it’s misused. You can stop at any time by replying to any of our emails.
  6. We keep your name, email, date of birth, your passkey (never your face or fingerprint) and a record of the games you covered, to run the programme. Ask us to delete them any time.
